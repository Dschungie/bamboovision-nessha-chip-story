A Nessha & Chip story

The Dragon in the
Bamboo Forest

One bright morning, Nessha followed a blue butterfly beyond the garden path.

Enter the forest ↓

01

Above the leaves

A shadow slipped out of the clouds. It had wings, horns—and a very empty stomach.

Dragon “Oh! What a delicious little child...”

02

Closer. Closer.

The dragon followed without a sound. But Nessha heard only birds, bamboo and the tiny song of the forest.

03

Then the sky went dark

The forest held its breath.

04

BOOM!

05

Leaves settled

The dragon landed in the softest pile of moss in the whole forest. And between it and Nessha stood someone small, wooden and very brave.

06

Chip “Hi. Do you need help?” “Be careful.”

07

Nessha “Yes. I don’t have anyone to play with.”
Chip “Okay. No problem.” “Let’s have fun.”

08

Up above the treetops

First, they borrowed the wind.

09

By the quiet water

They waited very quietly for a fish. The fish did not wait quietly at all.

10

Down the bamboo road

They raced the falling petals—and laughed all the way.

11

Where the river meets the sea

Nessha learned that waves are just hills that move.

12

When the sky turned peach

Nessha’s yawns grew bigger than dragon wings. Mom and Dad were waiting beneath the porch light.

13

Chip “See you tomorrow.”

Tomorrow was already waiting.
